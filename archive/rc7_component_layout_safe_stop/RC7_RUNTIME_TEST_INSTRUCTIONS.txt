EXPECTED COMPONENT PROBE
------------------------
Immediately after a unique physical root binds, inspect DIAG_COMPONENT.

A valid discovered layout must show:
  passed=true
  slot_count=N
  sampled_count=N
  reason=NONE
  layout_method=LEGACY_EXACT or DISCOVERED_PAIR_LEGACY_STATE or DISCOVERED_PAIR_DISCOVERED_STATE
  entity_component_offset=<number>
  component_backref_offset=<number>
  movement_state_offset=<number>

If it fails, preserve the complete line. RC7 now reports:
  pair_candidates=<count>
  pair_report=[entityOffset:backrefOffset,...]
  state_candidates=<count>
  state_report=[offset,...]
  reason=<specific failure>

Important fail-closed meanings:
- COMPONENT_LAYOUT_PAIR_NOT_FOUND: no stable Entity/component/backref pair in the bounded scan.
- COMPONENT_LAYOUT_PAIR_AMBIGUOUS: multiple structural pairs fit; RC7 refuses to guess.
- MOVEMENT_STATE_CANDIDATE_NOT_FOUND: pair found, no acceptable live state field.
- MOVEMENT_STATE_CANDIDATE_AMBIGUOUS: pair found, multiple state fields fit; RC7 refuses to guess.
- ENTITY_VTABLE_OR_ALIVE_SLOT_INVALID: layout may look structural, but one Entity/vt+0x630 target failed the read-only executable precheck.
